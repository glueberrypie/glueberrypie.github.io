Sun Mengtian works in installation and sound. She is interested in creative coding, knitting, digital painting and hardware hacking, and often uses the method of assemblage to create new works. Her work explores the relationship between digital technology and memory.

Untitled_2026

It is a suspended sound sculpture, and its sound is intentionally fragile. The transparent film acts as a vibrating membrane, turning the body of the work into the surface through which sound is transmitted. The work asks the viewer to move closer, shifting attention from loudness to proximity.

The sound is made by artist Shen Xiuyuan

Dimension: Variable
